## Full Description (max 4000 chars)
**NEON SKY SURVIVORS** — A fast-paced neon aircraft roguelite survival game.

Take control of a neon-powered plane and survive relentless waves of enemies for 10 pulse-pounding minutes. Every run is different: choose temporary weapon upgrades as you level up and build a unique combination that carries you to the final boss.

**TWO LAYERS OF PROGRESSION**

- **During the run** — level up and choose from 3 upgrade cards to build a temporary weapon loadout: plasma cannons, homing missiles, orbit blades, and laser wings. Combine them with passive boosts to unlock devastating weapon evolutions.

- **Between runs** — visit the Garage and permanently improve your aircraft by equipping and upgrading parts across 6 slots: Weapon, Wings, Engine, Hull, Core, and Radar. Merge three duplicate items to raise their rarity from Common to Legendary and beyond.

**FEATURES**

- One-hand touch controls — drag to move, tap to dash
- Auto-targeting main weapon fires continuously
- 6 enemy types with distinct behaviors: Chasers, Fast Wings, Shooter Drones, Shield Drones, Mine Carriers, and Splitter Orbs
- 3 boss encounters at 3:00, 6:00, and 10:00 — each harder than the last
- 26 equipment items across 5 rarity tiers (Common → Mythic)
- 4 weapon evolutions: Plasma Storm, Rocket Swarm, Solar Splitter, Neon Barrier
- 2 Mythic tier items: Void Engine and Storm Reactor
- Daily missions and account level progression
- Procedural neon synthwave soundtrack and sound effects
- Fully offline — no internet required, no ads, no in-app purchases

**CONTROLS**
- Drag anywhere on screen to move your plane
- Tap the DASH button to dash and leave a damaging neon trail
- Charge and release the SPECIAL button for the Neon Nova ultimate

Fight through escalating waves, defeat the Eclipse Core final boss, and build the most powerful neon aircraft ever flown.
