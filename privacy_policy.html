Neon Sky Survivors — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-06-03

1. Overview

Neon Sky Survivors ("the Game") is an offline mobile game. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The Game stores the following data locally on your device only, using Android's standard app-private storage:

  • Game progress (completed runs, best times, enemies defeated)
  • Equipment inventory and equipped loadout
  • Coin balance
  • Settings (music volume, SFX volume, vibration preference)
  • Crash and error logs stored in app-private storage (crash_log.txt)

This data never leaves your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

3. No Internet Permission

The Game does not request the INTERNET permission and cannot transmit any data over a network.

4. No Third-Party SDKs

The Game does not include any advertising, analytics, or social SDKs.

5. Children

The Game does not knowingly collect information from children or from anyone. No information is collected.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of the Game means you accept the updated policy.

7. Contact

Questions? Email us at support@neonsky.game.
